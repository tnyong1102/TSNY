## Priority 1: likely errors / breach risk

1. **CSR-29 Living Wages:** benchmark RM1,693/month (s.5.1.3) is below the RM1,700 minimum wage (s.6.1). Recalculate. Minimum wage should be a floor.
2. **CSR-29:** benchmark cites Numbeo Johor Bahru costs for a Nibong Tebal site.
3. **CSR-29 s.5.3:** gap bands overlap ("10-20% or <RM400" vs ">20% or >RM400"; RM400 ≈ 23.6% of RM1,693). Shortfalls under 10% are unclassified.
4. **CSR-29 s.4 vs s.5.3.1:** Total Pay includes statutory contributions, but net pay deducts statutory deductions. Separate employer contributions from employee deductions.
5. **CSR-30 s.7.1(b):** lists acid-lead batteries as not scheduled waste. Likely incorrect (spent lead-acid batteries appear as SW 104). Check First Schedule.
6. **CSR-30 s.7.4(f):** storage limit "180 days, or 20 tonnes." Verify wording and how the two limits combine.
7. **CSR-28:** four emergency response plans (fire, chemical spill, human injury, picket/strike) are appendices not included. Cannot verify s.5.4.3.

## Priority 2: conflicts between documents

- **Chemical spill drill:** CSR-28 s.5.7.6 every 2 years vs CSR-31 s.8 annual for water-related incidents including major chemical spills.
- **Energy policy:** CSR-32 has its own policy statement; ESG-02 Energy Management Policy covers the same ground. No cross-reference.
- **SDS:** CSR-28 s.5.9.1 "should be available" vs CSR-13 s.5.1 mandatory 16-section SDS in English and Bahasa Malaysia before arrival.
- **Role names (CSR-30):** Management Representative, EMR, HSE-HOD, HSE Manager used for the same role.
- **Reviewer title:** CSR-29 lists PS Lim as General Manager (same as approver). CSR-12 (2018) lists PS Lim as Admin HOD.
- **Recruitment fees:** ESG-03 "Employer Pays" vs ESG-04 "Supplier Pays" vs ESG-08 s.6.4 (employer pays).
- **Non-discrimination grounds:** lists differ across ESG-03, ESG-08, CSR-01, CSR-02. CSR-02 adds sexual orientation, gender identity, parental status.
- **Energy policy date:** ESG-02 text 1 April 2025, header 01/03/2025. Review cycle bi-annual vs annual elsewhere.
- **Competent person (electrical):** CSR-20 says Energy Commission (Suruhanjaya Tenaga). CSR-24 says JBEG (likely outdated; electricity functions moved to the Energy Commission).
- **Electrical PTW threshold:** CSR-24 definition "600V and above" vs s.5.1 "higher than 600V."
- **Work-at-height PTW:** CSR-17 requires PTW for all non-routine work at height. CSR-24 s.5.1 limits sub-permit to scaffolding and roof work.
- **Competent person (confined space):** CSR-27 CSCP is DOSH-certified. CSR-24 uses JBEG and DOSH references.
- **Permit retention:** CSR-27 entry permits 2 years; CSR-22 hot work permits 3 months; CSR-24 not stated; CSR-18 incident records 10 years.
- **Health declaration:** CSR-24 s.6 lists it as a document; CSR-27 does not mention it.
- **Risk assessment review cycle:** CSR-16 ergonomic 2 years vs CSR-21 annual.
- **Risk scoring:** CSR-21 (L x S, MEDIUM 5-14) vs CSR-10 (P x highest E, Significant 11-14). Bands do not align.
- **Safety role title:** CSR-20 "Health and Safety Manager" vs SH-C elsewhere.
- **Review periods:** CSR-22 hot work 2 years vs annual elsewhere.
- **Training procedure name:** CSR-24 "Training & Development Procedure" vs CSR-26 "Training Procedure."
- **Policy name:** CSR-26 "Company OSH, Health & Environment Policy" vs ESG-05 "Safety, Health & Environmental Protection Policy."
- **Hazard assessment owner:** CSR-25 s.6.3.1 (SH-C) vs CSR-21 s.5 (department heads).
- **Machine guard:** CSR-15 s.8 "safety officer" vs SH-C elsewhere.
- **Chemical waste:** CSR-13 s.5.5 names Hawk Filtration (SEA) Sdn Bhd for waste minimisation. An SOP should not commit a third party without a contract.
